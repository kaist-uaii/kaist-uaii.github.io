About

KAIST Urban AI (KUAI) is a research institute dedicated to reimagining cities as intelligent, adaptive systems through the convergence of artificial intelligence and urban science.

We conduct fundamental research and real-world deployments that integrate AI, data infrastructure, mobility, buildings, and human activity into coherent urban systems — where cities can learn, optimize, and continuously improve.

Artificial Intelligence is transforming the world. We focus on the next frontier — Urban Intelligence.

Urban Intelligence connects data, infrastructure, mobility, buildings, and human activity into an integrated system where cities can continuously learn and improve.

At KAIST Urban AI, we conduct research and real-world deployment of AI, integrating it into urban systems for sustainable, human-centered innovation.
