About the Symposium

The KAIST Symposium on Urban AI brings together researchers and practitioners studying artificial intelligence for complex urban systems. The symposium focuses on integrating sensing, machine learning, optimization, digital twins, and policy design to support resilient, efficient, and human-centered cities.

Call for Abstracts

Topics

We invite abstracts on foundational methods, urban systems, and human-centered applications of AI for cities.

  • Privacy, security, and governance of urban data
